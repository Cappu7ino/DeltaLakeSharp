# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- showing empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Report concerns through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that are not aligned with this code of conduct.
